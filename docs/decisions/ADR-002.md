# ADR-002: Keep Lead State Separate from Downstream Insurance Lifecycles

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-29

## Context

The conceptual master journey mentions policy issuance, renewal, and claim
support alongside Lead stages. Placing all of these on a Lead would make a
sales opportunity behave like a Customer, Policy, Renewal, and Claim at once.
Those modules are outside Release 1.

## Decision

Release 1 Lead state ends in `WON`, `LOST`, or `CANCELLED`.
`POLICY_ISSUED`, `RENEWAL_DUE`, and `CLAIM_SUPPORT` are not Lead states.
Quotation and payment-related Lead statuses may record observed sales progress
without creating deferred-module records. Future Customer conversion, Policy,
Renewal, and Claim workflows will own their records and reference the
originating Lead where appropriate.

Ownership is also separate from status: assignment moves `NEW` to `ASSIGNED`,
while transfer preserves the current sales status.

## Consequences

- A Lead remains a sales opportunity rather than becoming a lifecycle catch-all.
- Downstream modules can evolve with their own state machines.
- Release 1 can track useful progress without fabricating Quotation or Policy
  data.
- Future Lead-to-Customer conversion still requires an approved workflow and
  migration/integration design.

## Rejected Alternatives

- Put policy, renewal, and claim states on Lead: conflates business entities and
  creates ambiguous ownership/history.
- Treat assignment as a status reset on every transfer: destroys accurate
  sales progress.
- Auto-create incomplete downstream records from status changes: violates the
  boundaries of the approved Release 1 slice.
